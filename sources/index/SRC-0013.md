summary: >
  Leviathan is an official Project Moon webcomic/web novel serialized on POSTYPE, subtitled "The Epic of Vergilius
  the Red Gaze". The story bridges the gap between the true ending of Library of Ruina and the beginning of Limbus
  Company. It covers Vergilius's past as the Color Fixer "Red Gaze", his relationships with Garnet and Lapis, and
  the circumstances leading to his role as guide of the Limbus Company bus department. The webcomic portion ran
  from December 24, 2021 to June 17, 2022 (11 chapters illustrated by artist monggeu). After the artist withdrew
  for health reasons, the remaining chapters were released as a web novel with illustrations by Project Moon's
  internal art team, concluding on October 28, 2022 with a final chapter titled "Charon and Garnet". The English
  translation covers chapters 1-15; chapters 16-20 (final) remain untranslated. The series consists of 5 English
  posts, 13 Korean posts, and 7 Japanese posts on POSTYPE.
