summary: >
  Chapter 1 of The Distortion Detective introduces Moses, the protagonist who possesses the unique ability to visually
  perceive the Distortion phenomenon before it physically manifests. The chapter establishes the core methodology of
  her work: a four-step process of Cause, Explanation, Understanding, and Acknowledgement to resolve distortion cases.
  Two detailed case studies are presented in this chapter: Case #5 "Merfolk" (a villager from District 19 who
  transformed into a fish-person due to abuse-driven escapism) and Case #13 "Balloon People" (service industry workers
  in O Corp's Nest whose heads literally inflate and explode from accumulated stress). Moses explains that she sees
  every person as distorted to some degree—except herself in the mirror. The chapter establishes the tone: Moses is
  not a charity worker but a professional who investigates cases she is hired for, and her "solutions" often do not
  address the root causes of distortion. The series was published on POSTYPE and ran for 42 chapters before being
  discontinued, with Project Moon citing a preference to adapt the material into a future game.
