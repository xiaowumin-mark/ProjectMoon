summary: The City is divided into 26 districts, each governed by a Wing (mega-corporation designated A through Z). The Head, operating from A, B, and C Corps, rules as the supreme authority. A rigid social hierarchy emerges with Nests (privileged inner districts) and Backstreets (impoverished outer areas). The Fixer system, Associations, and Syndicates are established as key power structures within the City.

  - Infrastructure divides each district into Nest and Backstreet regions.
