summary: Carmen initiates the Seed of Light project, with the true goal of curing the "disease of the mind" afflicting all City dwellers. The project involves Angela, an AI created by the Manager, and the Sephirot (individuals who underwent transformation). The Manager (Ayin) enacts countless iterations and time loops within the facility, working toward the project's completion.
causes:
  - Carmen's desire to heal the psychological and spiritual sickness of the City.
  - The realization that Enkephalin production alone is not enough to achieve this goal.
process:
  - Carmen envisions the Seed of Light and begins the project.
  - Angela is created as an AI to manage the process across multiple iterations.
  - The Sephirot are created/transformed.
  - The Manager (Ayin) executes the plan through repeated loops, refining each iteration.
outcomes:
  - After countless cycles, the Seed of Light reaches completion.
  - The accumulated energy and Light are primed for release.
