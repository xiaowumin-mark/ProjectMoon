summary: The Light accumulated through the Seed of Light project is released from the Lobotomy Corporation facility. The Library manifests in the City. A period of "White Nights and Dark Days" follows, during which the Distortion phenomenon begins. Light spreads across the entire City, affecting countless lives and marking a major turning point in the setting.

  - The Library appears as a new entity within the City.
  - Individuals begin experiencing the Distortion phenomenon—hearing Carmen's voice and transforming.
  - Some instead manifest E.G.O., a different response to the Light.

  - The Lobotomy Corporation era ends.

  - Distortions become a widespread phenomenon across the City.
