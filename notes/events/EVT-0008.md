summary: Angela creates the Library, a mysterious location that stores knowledge and information in the form of books. The Library issues invitations to individuals across the City. Those who accept become guests and must engage in reception battles. Defeated guests are transformed into books, their knowledge and essence absorbed into the Library.
causes:
  - The release of the Light and manifestation of the Library during White Nights and Dark Days.
  - Angela's desire to collect knowledge and ultimately become human.
process:
  - The Library forms as a self-contained dimension/realm.
  - Invitations are sent out to potential guests.
  - Guests arrive and engage in structured reception battles across Library floors.
  - Defeated guests become books containing their knowledge and experiences.
outcomes:
  - The Library accumulates vast amounts of knowledge and power.
  - The City becomes aware of the Library's existence and its threat.
long_term_impact:
  - The collected books fuel Angela's transformation (EVT-0013).
